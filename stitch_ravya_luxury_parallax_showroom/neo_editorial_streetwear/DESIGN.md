## Brand & Style

This design system blends high-fashion editorial rigor with contemporary streetwear playfulness. It targets an audience of design-conscious tastemakers, Gen Z and millennial fashion curators, and boutique shoppers who view clothing as personal artistic expression. The emotional response is intentional, punchy, optimistic, and culturally plugged-in.

The design movement combines **Neo-Brutalist structural framing** with **Neo-Editorial digital layouts**:
- High-contrast typography paired with rounded container canvases.
- Tactile, playful sticker badges, metric callouts (`123,45K+`), and floating editorial pins.
- Clean white modular inner surfaces floating over soft mint and seafoam environments.
- High-saturation fashion photography blocks anchored by structured pill-shaped navigational controls.

## Colors

The palette uses cool, breezy seafoam and jade tones paired with warm, high-energy citrus accents:

- **Primary (`#2A9D8F`):** Deep seafoam teal used for brand anchors, primary action highlights, and active container fills.
- **Secondary (`#F4A261`):** Warm marigold tone for editorial tags, sticker accents, and secondary visual moments.
- **Tertiary (`#E76F51`):** High-energy tangerine used for urgency banners, interactive hot-spots, and promo indicators.
- **Neutral (`#191D1C`):** Deep carbon black for high-impact typography, dark pill buttons, and structural lines.
- **Canvas Base (`#D8ECE7`):** Soft seafoam mint foundation backing white inner canvas panels.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white modular cards providing crisp editorial isolation for photography.

## Typography

The type system pairs an expressive geometric display font with a clean, contemporary sans-serif:

- **Syne** commands headings, editorial titling, and numerical metrics. With bold character forms and tight tracking, it evokes print lookbooks and zines.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** delivers neutral, highly legible support for body copy, navigational anchors, and transactional e-commerce controls.
- **Inline Pill Treatments:** Headers frequently incorporate inline photography pills or contrasting weight color shifts (e.g., solid carbon paired with muted mint) within the same line.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is built on an expansive outer container with generous canvas margins.

- **Outer Enclosure:** Layouts rest inside a soft mint outer surround (`#D8ECE7`), framing the primary white canvas (`#FFFFFF`) with 16px to 32px of margin.
- **Desktop System:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid with 24px gutters. Visual blocks are arranged in asymmetric, editorial proportions (e.g., 5-column content card paired with a 7-column hero model shot).
- **Mobile System:** A 4-column grid collapsing side-by-side modules into stacked 24px-radius cards, with outer margins dropping to 16px to preserve media real estate.
- **Rhythm:** Internal component spacing follows standard 4px/8px baselines, with generous padding inside primary cards (`space-xl` / 40px) to mirror editorial print margins.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses layered flat surfaces, crisp geometry, and targeted atmospheric blurs rather than heavy drop shadows:

- **Canvas Isolation:** Primary depth is achieved by stacking: Mint Window Ground (`#D8ECE7`) → Elevated White Canvas Panel (`#FFFFFF`) → Modular Color Blocks (`#2A9D8F`, `#F4A261`, or image canvases).
- **Glassmorphic Hotspots & Overlays:** Floating product tags and lookbook markers use frosted glass overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) bound by 1px translucent borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Ambient Accent Shadows:** Product hover states use soft tinted glows (`0 12px 32px -8px rgba(42, 157, 143, 0.18)`) instead of murky neutral shadows.
- **Zero-Depth Structural Insets:** Floating search fields and capsule inputs sit flat within containers, relying on stark color contrast and pill silhouettes to create visual depth.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses exaggerated radii, pairing full pill forms with rounded modular frames:

- **Macro Containers:** Outer window shells and major feature cards carry large 28px–36px corners.
- **Internal Imagery & Panels:** Secondary photo cards and sub-containers use 20px–24px corners.
- **Micro Interactive Elements:** Buttons, text input capsules, filter chips, sticker tags, and floating price markers are fully rounded pills (`border-radius: 9999px`).
- **Inline Embedded Cutouts:** Inline text-image capsules mirror the full pill shape, sitting seamlessly within active typographic sentences.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action Pill:** Solid `#191D1C` background with `#FFFFFF` text, fully rounded (`9999px`), 14px uppercase/bold text, horizontal padding of 24px, accompanied by circular trailing icon discs.
- **Ghost Editorial Pill:** Transparent fill, 1.5px border (`#191D1C` or `#FFFFFF`), paired with circular directional action nodes (`↗`).
- **Accent Interactive Disc:** 40px–48px circular buttons used for search triggers, cart links, and quick-add actions.

### Badges, Tags & Editorial Stickers
- **Numerical Metric Badges:** Capsule cards in bold teal (`#2A9D8F`) or marigold (`#F4A261`) with crisp white Syne titling (`123,45K+`), paired with micro-labels.
- **Status & Collection Pills:** High-contrast translucent labels placed over product images (`CASUAL`, `BRIGHT`, `EXTREME`).

### Floating Price & Look Markers
- **Interactive Hotspot Marker:** 28px–34px semi-transparent white discs with micro price labels (`$203`, `$164`) anchored directly over garment coordinates on model imagery.
- **State Change:** Expands on hover into an editorial micro-card revealing item name, size availability, and quick-add actions.

### Input Fields
- **Capsule Inputs:** 52px height, full pill radius, `#F0F5F3` soft mint/neutral ground with no border, containing inset right-aligned solid pill submit buttons (`SUBSCRIBE`).

### Cards & Media Blocks
- **Editorial Lookbook Cards:** Asymmetrical split photo blocks with 24px corner radii, embedded metadata pills at base, and bold overlay typography.
- **Product Tiles:** Clean white surface backing full-bleed photography, rounded image corners (20px), subtle heart/wishlist iconography in top floating corners.
